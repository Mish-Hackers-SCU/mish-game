HOW TO PLAY

• The Samurai and Kunoichi take turns answering CS questions. A correct answer slashes the Demon "Normal" for 10 HP.

• Two correct answers in a row trigger a JOINT ATTACK — 20 HP, double damage.

• A wrong answer lets Normal strike the Heroes for 12 HP. Two wrong answers in a row unleash DEMON RAGE — 24 HP.

• If your HP falls to 1, use LAST WIND once per run — answer correctly to recover +25 HP.

• 20 questions total. Defeat Normal before the Heroes fall.
